Outdoors

Let’s come directly to IT’s antipodean: without giving the one or the other (IT or being outdoors) a preferment, outdoors is where I really like to be to recharge my biological battery and connect to nature again. Moreover, this is where I feel “real” and grounded (and more healthy), but the intellectual fascination of the IT-playground I am strongly fond of.

Creating Gear and Improving it

This is where creativity, technology and outdoors meet: give me any piece of technical or outdoor equipment and in a few moments I have some suggestions to improve it - or even worse - invent something which brings in completely new aspects.
As my engineering skills and budget are limited the quality of my self-developed gear never meets “really good” commercial equipment, but it is a huge fascination to do the full process from the problem to the first idea, some prototypes to the final product.

Have a look on my “developments” and ideas here: (Creating_Gear)[creating_gear]
