### Outdoors

Let's come directly to IT's antipodean: without giving the one or the other (IT or being outdoors) a preferment, outdoors is where I really like to be to recharge my biological battery and connect to nature again. This is where creativity, technology and outdoors meet: give me any piece of technical or outdoor equipment and in a few moments I have some suggestions to improve it - or even worse - invent something which brings in completely new aspects.\
